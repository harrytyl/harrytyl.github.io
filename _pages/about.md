I am a [Banting Postdoctoral Fellow](https://banting.fellowships-bourses.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html) in the [Quantitative Sciences Unit](https://med.stanford.edu/qsu.html), [Stanford University School of Medicine](https://med.stanford.edu/). I currently work with Dr. [Summer Han](https://med.stanford.edu/summerhanlab.html) on lung cancer research, leveraging large electronic health record data with data science, decision science, and health policy. My research interests include real-world data analysis, clinical prediction modeling, and decision-analytic modeling (e.g., reference policy model, value-of-information analysis), where my methodological research has always been driven by real-world applications.

Prior to my postdoc, I received my PhD in Quantitative Health Outcomes Research in the [Respiratory Evaluation Sciences Program](https://resp.core.ubc.ca/), Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences, [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/), under the supervision of Drs. [Mohsen Sadatsafavi](https://pharmsci.ubc.ca/directory/mohsen-sadatsafavi) and [John Petkau](https://www.stat.ubc.ca/users/john-petkau), and my MSc in Statistics under the supervision of Drs. [James V. Zidek](https://www.stat.ubc.ca/users/james-v-zidek-frsc-oc) and [Matias Salibian-Barrera](https://www.stat.ubc.ca/users/matias-salibian-barrera) at the same institution.
